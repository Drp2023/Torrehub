Paperwork

Getting your NIE in Torrevieja: what actually happens at the appointment

Elena Marín24 August 2026 · 7 min read

The NIE is the number everything else depends on — a bank account, a car, a rental contract, a phone line. The process itself is short. The preparation is where people lose weeks.

You book the appointment online through the Sede Electrónica, choosing the Torrevieja office rather than Alicante if you live south of Guardamar. Slots open in batches and disappear within hours, so check early in the morning rather than refreshing all afternoon.

What to bring

Three documents cover almost every case: the completed EX-15 form, your passport with a photocopy of the photo page, and the paid Modelo 790 fee receipt. The fee must be paid at a bank before you arrive — the office will not take payment.

The photocopy is the one people forget. There is no copier in the building, and the nearest shop closes at two.

Bring proof of why you need the number, too — a signed rental contract, a property reservation, an employment offer. Arriving without it is the most common reason for a wasted slot.

Why we built it

The Costa Blanca has a permanent population, a large expat community and millions of visitors a year — and almost no shared infrastructure connecting them. Local businesses advertise on paper. Landlords post in groups that vanish from search.

Torrehub brings ten categories into one index, all tied to one of 35 towns so a search means something geographically. Every seller account is checked by a person, and sellers can submit their NIE or NIF to earn a verified badge. No commission, no subscription — five free listings every 30 days.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 August 2026 · Terms uses this identical layout

1. Who we are 2. What we collect 3. Why we collect it 4. NIE / NIF documents 5. Cookies 6. Who we share with 7. Your rights 8. Contact

1. Who we are

Torrehub is a local directory and classifieds platform operating on the Costa Blanca, Spain. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what rights you have under the GDPR and Spanish data protection law.

2. What we collect

When you register we collect your name, username, email address and — for seller accounts — your NIE or NIF number. When you post a listing we store its contents, including photographs, contact details and location.

4. NIE and NIF documents

Verification documents are stored encrypted, visible only to reviewing administrators, and never displayed on your public profile or listings. Your NIE or NIF number itself is never shown publicly — only the verified badge is.
